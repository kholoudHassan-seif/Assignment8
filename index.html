Lasagna Bolognese

Layered Italian pasta with rich meat sauce and béchamel

Extended Preparation Time: This recipe requires more than 45 minutes to prepare. Plan accordingly!
  • 1 200g rice noodles
  • 2 200g shrimp, peeled
  • 3 2 eggs
  • 4 3 tablespoons tamarind paste
  • 5 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 6 1 tablespoon palm sugar
  • 7 Bean sprouts
  • 8 Crushed peanuts
  • 9 Lime wedges and cilantro
  1. 1 Soak rice noodles in warm water for 30 minutes. Drain and set aside.
  2. 2 Mix tamarind paste, fish sauce, and palm sugar to make the sauce.
  3. 3 Heat wok over high heat. Scramble eggs and set aside.
  4. 4 Cook shrimp until pink. Add noodles and sauce, toss for 2-3 minutes.
  5. 5 Add scrambled eggs and bean sprouts. Toss everything together.
  6. 6 Serve topped with crushed peanuts, lime wedges, and cilantro.
